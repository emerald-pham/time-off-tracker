# Time Off Tracker

Plans an IATSE Local 631 Regular Full Time cast member's time off against the Vacation and Floating Holidays they earn. Those come from Articles 20 and 21 of the STCU agreement, which Addendum B-6 leaves unchanged. With it, they can see what's left for future Occasions at any point in the year.

## Language

### Planning time off

**Occasion**:
A named stretch of dates someone is taking off, with a start date and an end date.
_Avoid_: Trip, event, request, vacation

**Day Off**:
One calendar date inside an Occasion.
_Avoid_: Vacation day, PTO day

**Day Type**:
What a Day Off is: RDO, Floating Holiday, Vacation or Unpaid.

**RDO**:
A Day Off that is one of the week's two days off (weeks run Sunday to Saturday), so it uses no benefit time. Which dates are RDO isn't a fixed weekday: the weekly rule puts them on the far side of the week break the time off crosses (US-25).
_Avoid_: Weekend, regular day off

**Unpaid**:
A Day Off taken without pay, so it uses no benefit time.
_Avoid_: LOA, unpaid leave

**Approved**:
Describes a Day Off the company has signed off on. An Occasion is Approved when every Day Off is, "Not approved yet" when none is, and "Partly approved" otherwise. Approved or not, a Day Off Burns the same Benefit Days.
_Avoid_: Confirmed, booked

**PTO Days**:
The number of Days Off in an Occasion whose Day Type is Floating Holiday or Vacation.
_Avoid_: Days of PTO required

**Season**:
The part of the year an Occasion starts in: Winter (Nov–Jan), Spring (Feb–Apr), Summer (May–Jul) or Autumn (Aug–Oct), labelled with the year of the season's first month.

### Benefit time

**Benefit Day**:
One 8-hour unit of earned Vacation or Floating Holiday, shown as one row on the Ledger.
_Avoid_: PTO, benefit time, hours

**Benefit Type**:
Which kind of benefit time a Benefit Day is: Vacation or Floating Holiday.

**Vacation**:
Benefit time earned by hours worked (Article 21). It carries over between calendar years.
_Avoid_: Using "vacation" for an Occasion or for time off in general

**Floating Holiday**:
Benefit time credited on fixed posting dates (Article 20, Section 3). It must be used in the calendar year it is credited.
_Avoid_: Personal holiday, FH, FLHOL

**Earn Date**:
The date a Benefit Day becomes usable. For Vacation credited before the eligibility date (nine months after hire), this is the eligibility date, not the date the hours were credited, whatever calendar year they were credited in.
_Avoid_: Accrual date, posting date

**Earned**:
Describes a Benefit Day whose Earn Date is on or before today.

**Estimated**:
Describes a Benefit Day whose Earn Date is after today, so it is projected and not yet earned. The opposite of Earned.
_Avoid_: Using "estimated" for an Earn Date that is only approximate

**Approximate**:
Describes an Earn Date assigned by the SAP walk-back, or beyond what the schedule explains, rather than known. Shown with "*".
_Avoid_: Estimated

**Accrual Tier**:
The yearly rate Vacation is earned at (1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks), set by which hire anniversary falls in the calendar year.

**Vacation Cap**:
Twice the yearly Vacation at the current Accrual Tier. While the balance is at the cap, no Vacation is earned, and the missed days are never earned back.

**Carryover**:
Vacation earned in an earlier calendar year that is still unused.
_Avoid_: Rollover

**Ledger**:
The list of Benefit Days, one per row, sorted by Earn Date and grouped by calendar year.
_Avoid_: Bank, balance sheet

### Using benefit time

**Burn**:
To use up a Benefit Day by assigning it to a Day Off of the same Benefit Type.
_Avoid_: Spend, allocate, deduct

**Clash**:
A date that two overlapping Occasions both mark as Vacation or Floating Holiday. Overlaps are otherwise allowed. A Clash is not.
_Avoid_: Conflict, double booking

**Short**:
Describes an Occasion that has more Vacation or Floating Holiday Days Off than there are eligible Benefit Days to Burn.

**Expired**:
Describes a Floating Holiday not Burned by December 31 of the year it was credited.

### SAP

**SAP Balance**:
The Vacation and Floating Holiday hours SAP shows as available, together with the date they were read.
_Avoid_: Current balance, bank

**Payroll Week**:
Sunday through Saturday. Its paycheck comes out on the Thursday after it ends.

**Deducted**:
Describes a Day Off whose Payroll Week's paycheck has come out, so the SAP Balance already reflects it.
